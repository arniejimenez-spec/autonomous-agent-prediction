# V9: Conservative Full-Data Refit Bagging

V9 preserves the official-0.822 v8.1 execution path and adds a narrowly routed way to use every labeled row. The change is deliberately small: at most two experimental predictions can enter the ten-candidate frontier, and the historical v8.1 train-CV winner remains the explicit hedge.

## Motivation

V8.1's test predictions are averages of cross-validation fold models. With four folds, each model sees only 75% of the labeled rows. A full-data refit can recover signal, but it has no honest out-of-fold score and can easily overfit or crowd proven candidates out of the submission cap.

V9 controls those risks:

- keep every historical v8.1 model and ensemble unchanged;
- only activate the new route for datasets with 1,000–30,000 rows;
- add one second-seed CV bag of the strongest individual model when the dataset has at most 15,000 rows;
- refit only the strongest individual model on all labeled rows;
- blend that full-data prediction at 20% into the strongest historical ensemble;
- reserve at most two frontier slots for v9 predictions;
- keep the strongest historical candidate, rather than the repeated-CV variant, as `CV_HEDGE`;
- wrap every extra fit in failure recovery and retain the v8.1 fallback modes.

The lower bound excludes tiny datasets where repeated-CV rankings were especially unstable. The single-model, single-weight rule was chosen after a broader pilot caused experimental variants to displace useful historical candidates.

## Evidence

The final fast-mode replay executes the exact tightened agent on all sixteen solved tasks. Under the official two-selection policy approximation—highest inner-public candidate plus the explicit historical CV hedge—v9 produces:

- v8.1 ten-candidate mean selected-private AUC: **0.805725**;
- v9 mean selected-private AUC: **0.805892**;
- mean delta: **+0.000167**;
- regressions: **0 of 16**.

The clearest gains are:

- `train_14`: +0.000946;
- `train_16`: +0.000925;
- `train_10`: +0.000421;
- `train_11`: +0.000358.

`train_06` adds +0.000019; the other eleven tasks are unchanged under the selection policy.

The completed replay is stored in `submissions/11_full_refit_bagging_v9/meta_results_all.json`. Reproduce the comparison with:

```powershell

  submissions/11_full_refit_bagging_v9/meta_results_all.json `
  --baseline-results submissions/09_meta_routed_automl_v8/meta_results_all.json
```

## Runtime safety

- Candidate files remain `p01.csv` through `p10.csv`.
- Completed replays produced 7–10 modeled candidates.
- Worst-case stdout remains 135 characters.
- The orchestration still requires no more than 14 tool calls.
- The initial sample submission, fast retry, fallback retry, and immediate final selection remain unchanged.
- The historical hedge is explicitly located after final candidate sorting, so experimental proxy scores cannot silently replace it.
