# V14 - Orthogonal Direct-Model Finalist

V14 treats the two final selection slots as a portfolio rather than two estimates of the same public optimum. The first slot is always the leakage-safe p01 CV hedge. The second slot is reserved for a public-competitive direct model whose predictions disagree with p01.

## Motivation

V13 submitted a broad Stage 1 portfolio and eight public-guided blends, but its official score remained 0.822. The refined public leader was still generally close to the historical ensemble frontier. If p01 already captures that frontier, selecting another correlated blend wastes the second private-evaluation slot.

V14 therefore removes Stage 2 entirely. It uses disagreement to buy upside while keeping p01 as the safety anchor.

## Selection rule

1. Fit the unchanged V13 Stage 1 model portfolio.
2. Save p01 as the strongest train-only CV hedge.
3. For every direct-model submission, compute `1 - correlation(rank(prediction), rank(p01))`.
4. Submit all p01-p13 candidates.
5. Find the best public score among direct models only.
6. Admit direct models within 0.005 AUC of that score.
7. Select the admitted model with the highest printed disagreement; break ties by public score and then candidate order.
8. Select p01 and the orthogonal direct model.

Ensembles and baseline-safety blends may be submitted for diagnostic coverage but cannot occupy the orthogonal slot.

## Budget and failure safety

| Component | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Initial fallback | 1 submission |
| Modeled candidates | 13 submissions |
| Total | 14 of 30 submissions |
| Final selections | 2 |

The first tool call submits `sample_submission.csv`. If normal modeling fails, the agent retries `--fast` and then `--fallback`. If p01 succeeds, it is always one of the final selections.

## Evidence

The saved V13 approximation shows the expected tradeoff: the forced direct finalist is about 0.00058 worse on mean selected-private AUC, but p01 absorbs many individual losses. The point of V14 is not local mean optimization; it is to make the second hidden finalist materially different.

Two exact V14 controls were run:

| Dataset | Orthogonal finalist | Diversity | p01 private | Orthogonal private | Selected private |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Train 13 | shallow CatBoost | 0.0717 | 0.649242 | 0.650786 | 0.650786 |
| Train 09 | logistic | 0.2225 | 0.651438 | 0.639511 | 0.651438 |

Train 13 demonstrates upside from the direct slot. Train 09 demonstrates downside containment by p01. On the saved Train 11 predictions, LightGBM is the only direct model inside the 0.005 public window and raises selected private AUC from 0.825635 to 0.826487.

The Train 13 stdout plan was 395 characters, safely below the harness's 500-character capture cap.
