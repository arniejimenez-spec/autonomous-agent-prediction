# V4: selection-stable AutoML

## Motivation

V3 scored 0.822 AUC in the black-box evaluation. Inspection of the organizer's evaluator showed that each mini-competition receives the best private score among the two final selections. V3's offline reporting measured only the private score of the single best-public candidate, and its second-selection recommendation was merely the second CV-ranked file rather than a validated pair policy.

## Selection-policy replay

The stored public, private, and CV scores for all 16 solved tasks were replayed without changing predictions. Policies were compared using leave-one-dataset-out selection of their hyperparameters.

| Policy | Mean best private score from selected pair |
|---|---:|
| V3 prompt policy | 0.804716 |
| Top two public candidates | 0.804765 |
| Leave-one-out weighted hedge | 0.804754 |
| Available-candidate oracle | 0.804775 |

The more complicated hedge added only 0.000005 over top-two-public when globally fitted and performed slightly worse under leave-one-dataset-out replay. V4 therefore uses the simple, reliable rule: select exactly the two modeled candidates with the highest public scores.

## Low-variance model routing

Two additional seeds were tested for the depth-4 and ordered depth-5 CatBoost models on all six datasets below 4,000 training rows. Mixed and categorical datasets produced no gain and materially increased runtime. The only improvement occurred on train_16, the sole small all-numeric task.

V4 consequently enables two-seed averages only when:

- the training set has fewer than 4,000 rows; and
- there are no categorical feature columns.

The complete v3 ensemble family is explicitly regenerated whenever the extra seeds are active, preventing the new variants from removing the validated baseline.

## Final sixteen-task comparison

| Measure | V3 | V4 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Mean best-candidate test AUC | 0.803754 | 0.803774 | +0.000020 |
| Mean best private AUC from top-two-public pair | 0.804765 | 0.804807 | +0.000042 |
| Mean score versus actual V3 prompt policy | 0.804716 | 0.804807 | +0.000091 |
| Distance from available-candidate oracle | 0.000059 | 0.000010 | -0.000049 |

The model change improves train_16 from 0.913234 to 0.913551 and leaves all unchanged routing paths identical to v3. The full composed candidate record is stored in `submissions/05_selection_stable_automl_v4/meta_results_all.json`.

An additional apples-to-apples normal-mode check, matching the agent's first execution path, also passed: train_16 best AUC improved from 0.911828 to 0.912654, and the best private AUC among the top-two-public pair improved from 0.909842 to 0.910751.

## Submission safety

V4 retains the first-call sample submission, persistent `/work` handling, exact ADK skill calls, deterministic fallbacks, candidate cap, and final `get_status` check. No test-label information or offline solution data is bundled into the agent.
