# V12 — OOF-Gated Count-View Specialist

V12 preserves the complete runtime-safe v8.1/V11 baseline and adds one isolated representation lane for medium-cardinality integer counts. The new lane cannot alter any historical model, blend, or hedge. It is exposed only when its best candidate beats the complete baseline hedge by at least 0.0006 leakage-safe out-of-fold AUC.

## Why V12 changed direction

V11 returned the same official **0.822 AUC** as v3 through v10. V12 therefore began with explicit bottleneck tests rather than another unconditional model addition.

- Candidate selection was not the bottleneck: expanding the saved v8 frontier from 10 to 28 candidates increased the private oracle ceiling by only **0.000080 mean AUC**.
- Cross-fitted Ridge and logistic stacks lost to the historical hedge on four representative tasks.
- A constrained forward rank blend gained OOF AUC on `train_09` but lost roughly **0.00338 private AUC**, demonstrating blend-weight overfitting.
- `row_id` hex fragments and periodic transforms stayed near random on held-out test labels.
- Quantile-binned additive logistic models, public weight sweeps, and a wider numeric XGBoost route did not provide repeatable gains.

The remaining structural gap was semantic: integer counts with 21–150 distinct values were treated only as ordered numeric values. V12 gives those columns a second categorical view, but only inside a separate specialist lane.

## Final architecture

1. Run the unchanged baseline portfolio and construct its exact ten-candidate frontier.
2. Detect numeric columns that are integer-like and have 21–150 distinct values.
3. On datasets with at least two such columns, 1,000–20,000 training rows, and sufficient time remaining, fit three count-view CatBoost variants: depth 4, ordered depth 5, and depth 6.
4. Build count-only blends plus controlled blends with the already-fitted logistic, spline, LightGBM, and Random Forest predictions.
5. Compare the best count-lane OOF AUC with the complete historical hedge.
6. Emit at most four count candidates only when the margin is at least **+0.0006 AUC**.
7. If the count lane is admitted, select the highest-public baseline and highest-public count candidate. Otherwise retain the historical public-leader-plus-CV-hedge policy.

The first submission remains `sample_submission.csv`, so a valid prediction exists even if modeling fails. Compact filenames keep the complete plan under the harness stdout cap.
