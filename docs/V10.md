# V10: Train-CV-Gated Neural Specialist

V10 changes model class rather than tuning another blend. It preserves the official-0.822 runtime-safe baseline and replaces v9's non-transferring refit candidates with one regularized multilayer perceptron (MLP) over the original dense one-hot representation.

## Motivation

V3 through v9 repeatedly reached 0.822 despite changes to CatBoost depth, Random Forest, XGBoost, quadratic interactions, DGP probes, repeated CV, and full-data refits. V9's small local replay gain did not transfer to the hidden evaluation.

An offline missing-family audit tested:

- shrinkage linear discriminant analysis;
- regularized quadratic discriminant analysis;
- Gaussian-random-projection histogram boosting;
- Gaussian-random-projection ExtraTrees;
- random Fourier features with logistic regression;
- a regularized dense one-hot MLP.

The projection, discriminant, and random-feature models were rejected because their train-CV and solved-test performance remained below the established portfolio. The MLP was the only specialist that beat every established individual model on a solved task using train-only evidence.

## Admission policy

The historical v8.1 candidate family is constructed first and remains unchanged. V10 then:

1. considers the MLP only for 1,000–20,000 training rows and at most 35 original features;
2. uses only original columns, avoiding tree-oriented duplicate categorical and ordinal views;
3. fits a `(64, 32)` ReLU network with strong L2 regularization and early stopping;
4. evaluates it with the same leakage-safe stratified folds as the established models;
5. admits one standalone MLP candidate only if its OOF AUC is at least 0.001 above the best established individual model;
6. keeps the strongest historical ensemble as `CV_HEDGE`.

The MLP never enters historical blends and cannot replace the hedge. A failed or slow neural fit is isolated, and the normal v8.1 frontier remains available.

## Evidence

The exact fast-mode v10 agent was replayed on all sixteen solved tasks.

- v8.1 mean selected-private AUC: **0.805725**;
- v10 mean selected-private AUC: **0.805725**;
- regressions: **0 of 16**;
- MLP admissions: **1 of 16**.

On `train_02`:

- best established individual-model CV AUC: **0.967993**;
- MLP CV AUC: **0.971823**;
- admission margin: **+0.003830**;
- MLP inner-public AUC: **0.976496**;
- historical hedge inner-public AUC: **0.975516**.

The MLP becomes the public-frontier choice while the historical hedge preserves the selected-private score. This is intentional option value: local best-of-two performance does not change, but the second model family is materially different when train-only evidence supports it.


## Runtime safety

- Candidate files remain `p01.csv` through `p10.csv`.
- Completed replays produced 7–10 modeled candidates.
- Worst-case stdout was 136 characters.
- The neural probe is skipped after 20 minutes of elapsed script time.
- The initial sample submission, compact stdout, ten-candidate cap, failure retries, and immediate final selection are unchanged.
