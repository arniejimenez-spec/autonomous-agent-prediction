# V11: Strictly Gated Equation Discovery

V11 preserves the official-0.822 runtime-safe portfolio and replaces V10's non-transferring MLP with explicit synthetic-equation feature scouts. The new models are not trusted by default: they are isolated from every historical ensemble and can expose at most one candidate after beating the already-built historical hedge in leakage-safe out-of-fold evaluation.

## Motivation

V3 through V10 all scored 0.822 despite increasingly diverse changes. V10's MLP cleared its train-CV gate on one solved dataset and led that dataset's inner-public split, but the official score still did not move. That result argues against admitting new families after a small comparison with only the best individual model.

V11 raises the standard in two ways:

1. probe candidate representations across all sixteen solved datasets before integrating them;
2. compare the final scout blend with the strongest complete historical ensemble, not merely with one established model.

## Offline feature-family audit

The development probe evaluates two shuffled three-fold CV seeds and solved-test transfer for:

- robustly scaled unary primitives: raw, absolute, square, signed square root, signed logarithm, hyperbolic tangent, and missingness;
- pairwise primitives: product, absolute difference, two bounded ratios, radial distance, and a bounded sine of the product;
- quantile-bin logistic regression.

No standalone feature scout beats the historical hedge in train CV on the sixteen solved tasks. The closest gaps are:

- `train_08`: -0.001160 AUC;
- `train_03`: -0.001495 AUC;
- `train_01`: -0.002982 AUC.

This rejects an always-on feature model. The complete probe record is stored in `submissions/13_equation_discovery_v11/feature_probe_results.json` and can be reproduced with `scripts/probe_v11_feature_discovery.py`.

## Runtime admission policy

The established V8.1 candidate family is built first and remains unchanged. V11 then:

1. considers scouts only for 1,000–15,000 rows, at most 35 original features, and 1–24 original numeric features;
2. skips tiny, large, all-categorical, and high-dimensional tasks;
3. fits unary and pairwise equation logistic models with the same stratified folds used by the established portfolio;
4. selects the stronger scout using out-of-fold AUC;
5. tests fixed 20%, 35%, and 50% scout weights against the strongest established individual model;
6. admits one blend only if it beats the complete historical hedge by at least 0.0015 OOF AUC;
7. permits a standalone scout only under the still stricter +0.004 margin;
8. preserves the pre-scout historical hedge as `CV_HEDGE`.

The equation candidate cannot enter or alter any historical blend. A failed scout is isolated, and fallback execution omits the entire experiment.

## Development evidence

The exact fast-mode V11 agent is replayed only on the eight solved tasks eligible for its route. The other eight tasks inherit the unchanged V8.1 execution path. Results are composed into `submissions/13_equation_discovery_v11/meta_results_all.json` and compared with:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe scripts/replay_v11_policy.py `
  submissions/13_equation_discovery_v11/meta_results_all.json `
  --baseline-results submissions/09_meta_routed_automl_v8/meta_results_all.json
```

The final policy replay reports:

- V8.1 mean selected-private AUC: **0.805725**;
- V11 mean selected-private AUC: **0.805725**;
- mean delta: **0.000000**;
- regressions: **0 of 16**;
- scouts attempted: **8 of 16**;
- scouts admitted: **0 of 16**.

`train_03` is the closest case: the best scout blend improves on the strongest individual model, but exceeds the historical hedge by only **+0.000779 AUC** and correctly fails the precommitted +0.0015 gate. This zero-admission result is intentional. The solved datasets do not justify changing their predictions, while a hidden task with materially stronger equation evidence retains a new option.

## Runtime safety

- The initial sample submission remains mandatory.
- Candidate files remain `p01.csv` through `p10.csv`.
- At most one V11 candidate can enter the frontier.
- The ten-candidate cap and compact stdout protocol are unchanged.
- The equation route starts only before 20 minutes of script time.
- Fast and fallback recovery modes remain available.
- Final selection still pairs the highest inner-public candidate with the explicit historical hedge.
- Completed routed replays emitted exactly 135 stdout characters.
- The slowest routed fast-mode replay took 507.9 seconds.
